Size Guide

All measurements in centimetres (cm)  ·  Unisex sizing

HOXOBIL pieces run true to size. Measure yourself and compare to the charts below. When in doubt, size up — our cuts are designed with a relaxed streetwear fit.

How to Measure

  • Chest — Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape horizontal.
  • Waist — Measure around your natural waistline.
  • Hips — Measure around the fullest part of your hips.
  • Length — Measure from the top of the shoulder to the hem.

Fit Guide

  • Oversized fit — size up 1–2 sizes from your measured size.
  • Regular fit — go with your measured size.
  • Slim fit — size down if you're between sizes.

Product pages indicate the fit style for each drop.
