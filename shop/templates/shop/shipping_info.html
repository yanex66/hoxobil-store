Shipping Info

Fulfilled by Printful  ·  Worldwide Delivery

Every HOXOBIL piece is printed and shipped on demand by Printful. Here's everything you need to know about how your order gets to you.

How It Works

When you place an order, it is sent directly to Printful's production facility. Your item is printed, quality-checked, and packaged — then handed to a carrier for delivery. This process typically takes 2–7 business days before your order even ships.

Estimates are business days and do not include weekends, public holidays, or customs delays.

Shipping Costs

Shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on your location, the items in your cart, and the selected shipping method. We offer free shipping on orders over $75.

Tracking Your Order

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number. You can track your shipment from your Order History page. Click into any order and select "Track Order" for live updates.

Customs & Import Duties

International orders may be subject to customs duties, taxes, or import fees imposed by your country. These charges are the buyer's responsibility and are not included in our prices or shipping costs. HOXOBIL has no control over these fees.

Lost or Delayed Packages

If your package has not arrived within the estimated timeframe, please first check your tracking link. If the package appears lost, contact us at help.hoxobil@gmail.com and we will investigate with Printful on your behalf.

Wrong Address

Please double-check your shipping address before placing an order. Once an order is submitted to Printful, we cannot change the delivery address. HOXOBIL is not responsible for orders shipped to an incorrectly entered address.
